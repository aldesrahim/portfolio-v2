A tax status checker for Pesawaran regency in Lampung. Citizens look up what they owe online instead of going to the tax office. It is one of several e-tax applications I built and maintained for local governments across several regencies.

This one runs on Laravel and Livewire. The rest of the suite is older: the core e-tax system runs on PHP 5.6 with a Java service for tax calculation, and a second checker runs on CodeIgniter 3. I maintained and modernized both while building the newer ones.
