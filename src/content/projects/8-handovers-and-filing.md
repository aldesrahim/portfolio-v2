A shipment and document tracker for a friend's fulfillment operation. It records incoming and outgoing shipments by air waybill (AWB) and keeps the paperwork attached to each one. Operators write a handover log at the end of a shift, so the next shift knows what happened.

Each document carries its own filing status. Built with Laravel Jetstream and Livewire, so the screens update without a page reload.
